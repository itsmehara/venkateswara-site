About the shop

Run by the man
who does the work

Sri Venkateswara Screen & Textile Printing Works is Venkatesh's shop in Anakapalle. He takes the enquiry, quotes the job and sees it out of the door.

The workshop

Stitched here.
Printed here.

Most printing shops send the stitching out, and most tailors send the printing out. Between the two, an order can sit for a week while each waits on the other.

Here both happen in the same workshop. Cloth is cut and stitched to your measurements, and the branding — screen printed or embroidered — goes on in the same building. One person is answerable for the whole job.

The hat above came off the stitching table that morning. It is shown blank on purpose: that is the point at which your logo goes on.
