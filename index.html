Bulk uniforms

Stitched to measure.
Branded to match.

Most shops either stitch or print. Venkatesh does both, which is why a uniform order here does not get handed between two places that never speak to each other.

You give the sizes and the logo. The cloth is cut and stitched to those measurements, your mark goes on — embroidered or screen printed, whichever suits the fabric and the budget — and the whole order comes back counted, checked and packed.
